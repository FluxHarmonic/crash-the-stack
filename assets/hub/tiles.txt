# Crash The Stack hub tiles (rulings D60 and D61): the world drawn from
# small hand-defined bitmaps, 16x16 cells drawn at 2x (32 px on the
# 640x400 view, a 20x11 window centered on the runner), text-art like
# the font, the glyph sheets and the credits. Edit this file, not the
# module. scripts/art/hub-tiles turns it into src/crash/hub/tiles.sgl
# and assets/hub/tiles.png.
#
# A cell is . (transparent) or 1..8, an entry of the ramp below, eight
# palette roles from dark to bright with two accents (the teal wire for
# the runner and the data flow, the ICE orange for the ICE and the
# daemons' eyes). The game lights a tile in four steps and six hues
# ((crash hub draw)'s DIM and HUES: every step and hue is still a
# palette role); dithered, no alpha (the style bible).
#
# The plane is opaque (the field shows only through void, under the
# veil); an item tile is drawn over the plane with its ground
# transparent; a barrier is a lit top face over a dark front face (when
# the cell below is walkable) with a shadow row on the plane below;
# nodes, gates and the market are small cabinets the same way; a cell
# never seen is fog. The runner and the daemon are drawn in the block
# alphabet's vocabulary: full blocks, glyphs first, icons later.
#
# A tile opens with "= name" and is followed by exactly sixteen rows of
# sixteen cells.
