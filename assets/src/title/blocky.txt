# The scene's vocabulary, shapes only: █ full, ▀ top half, ▄ bottom half,
# ▌ left half, ▐ right half, space. Every glyph is 6 rows of 7 cells; the
# script adds the cell between letters, the shade ramp (░▒▓█ across the
# body), the two-tone split, the drop shadow and the colors, so one
# alphabet gives every style on the candidates sheet. Vertical strokes two
# cells wide, horizontal strokes one row, corners rounded with the half
# blocks. A glyph starts with "= X" (one uppercase letter, a digit, : or /) and is followed
# by exactly six rows; a row shorter than 7 is padded with spaces.
