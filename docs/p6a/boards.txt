SCAN (P6a): guess-free boards, per preset

Generated by docs/p6a/boards-sheet.sgl from (crash scan generate) at the
commit that added this file or changed it last. Each board is a function
of (seed, uplink): the ICE is placed after the first click, never in its
3x3. Where the solver gets stuck, ICE around the stuck spot is moved
(a perturbation) and the board is solved again from the uplink, until it
clears without a guess (D68). `perturbations` counts the moves (a fresh
placement is dealt when nothing can be moved); 0 and 0: the first placement cleared as dealt.

Steps read `scan x,y (rule)` or `flag x,y (rule)`, x from the left and y
from the top, both from 0. The rules:
  uplink  the first click
  a       a reading whose flags equal it: its other hidden neighbors are clean
  b       a reading whose missing ICE equal its hidden neighbors: all ICE
  c       one reading's hidden set inside another's: the difference is settled
  d       the global count: no ICE left, or as many ICE as hidden hosts
A scan of a zero opens its area (the flood); floods are not listed.
`the checker` is (crash scan check), which sees only what the player
would and proves each step forced by enumeration.
