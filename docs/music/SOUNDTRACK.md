labels remain useful as descriptions and in the older source filenames, but
they are not the track names. This is a working selection, not a mastered or
published album. The order below is a possible listening sequence.

The reusable authoring, timing, rendering and listening-review workflow is in
[AUDIO-REVIEW-PROCESS.md](AUDIO-REVIEW-PROCESS.md), including the limits of

With the approved Fault Line addition, this makes **16 distinct compositions**, counting each legacy piece once and
not counting alternate mixes as new songs. David accepted Quiet Array and
Dead Sector enthusiastically, including both focused sections, with no changes
requested; see [PUZZLE-PAIR.md](PUZZLE-PAIR.md). The last two planned originals,
Obsidian Index and Clock Edge, now have full calm/tense auditions;
see [FINAL-PAIR.md](FINAL-PAIR.md). David likes both, but questions Obsidian's
similarity to other songs and requests more body/ambience in Clock Edge.
[CLOCK-ROOM-II.md](CLOCK-ROOM-II.md) records the score comparison with Quiet
Array and the separate Clock Edge ambience audition. David accepted Clock Edge
II as a good balance between the quieter tunes and more rhythm and motion.
Keep II as its reference. The authorized Obsidian rework is a separate Pedal II
audition; see [OBSIDIAN-II.md](OBSIDIAN-II.md). Retain both originals.
David accepted Obsidian II as great and definitely unique, then requested
wavier bass and a delicate wider pad. Waves III was too subtle to hear;
Waves IV raises the side pad and strengthens the moving upper bass layer.
See [OBSIDIAN-III.md](OBSIDIAN-III.md) and [OBSIDIAN-IV.md](OBSIDIAN-IV.md).
David approves IV's audible pad support and stronger distinction from Quiet
Array. Keep IV as the current reference; no further refinements are requested.

David also commissioned a bold Black Glass title theme with demoscene-style
FM character. `docs/music/alternates/black-glass/black-glass-title.cts` is a separate 2:03 arrangement with
a new metallic FM hook, early grit entrance and a repeating menu body. See
[BLACK-GLASS-TITLE.md](BLACK-GLASS-TITLE.md). Keep the accepted Grit gameplay
version unchanged; this title audition is not a sixteenth composition.
David likes its softer passage but finds the chirpy dirty bass too loud.
Main Theme II lowers only that voice by about 4 dB and adds five brief FM
arpeggios at phrase endings; see [BLACK-GLASS-TITLE-II.md](BLACK-GLASS-TITLE-II.md).
David likes the improved title balance but requests removal of those lead
flourishes. Main Theme III restores the first lead and retains II's quieter
grit. His optional idea was a separate rapid arpeggiated stab; III adds none.
See [BLACK-GLASS-TITLE-III.md](BLACK-GLASS-TITLE-III.md).
David then confirmed the rapidly cycling tracker chord and requested that
old-school flavor. `black-glass-title-v4.cts` adds twelve short pulse-wave
chord stabs, preserving III's melody, grit balance and softer bridge.
See [BLACK-GLASS-TITLE-IV.md](BLACK-GLASS-TITLE-IV.md). David accepted IV: its tracker chord stabs establish the modern-plus-retro
identity. The shipping title arrangement is `assets/tunes/black-glass-title.cts`,
with the display title **Black Glass / Main Theme**. III remains a historical
balance reference.

The first drum-kit comparisons are now `sector-drift-dust-kit.cts` and

See [DRUM-KITS.md](DRUM-KITS.md).

The next percussion comparisons were `cold-boot-machine-kit.cts` and
`basement-circuit-signal-kit.cts`. They add a mechanical knock/blip character
to Cold Boot and a boxier kit with sparse root-tuned beeps/toms to Basement
Circuit. David liked both kits, but found Cold Boot's fill conspicuously
different and noticed Basement's occasional retained snare. Separate II
auditions match Cold Boot's fill/ghost voice to its C4 main knock and gently
shorten and soften Basement's snare noise and reverb. David accepted both drum
revisions: Cold Boot's fill is much more appropriate, and Basement's snare
now blends without jumping out. Keep Cold Boot II as the selected reference;
Basement III retains II's drums and reduces its transition swell by 4.08 dB
before the bus. The first kits and earlier references remain available.
See [MACHINE-SIGNAL-KITS.md](MACHINE-SIGNAL-KITS.md)
and [KIT-FILLS-II.md](KIT-FILLS-II.md).
